ABOUT TRM

Teachers for Ruto Movement (TRM) is a voluntary network of serving teachers, retired teachers and teacher trainees united in support of H.E. Dr. William Samoei Ruto's re-election for a second term in 2027.

We believe that the progress made under his leadership deserves recognition and continuity. Our mission is to mobilise teachers across Kenya, promote awareness of the government's achievements, strengthen unity among supporters and champion the case for a second term in 2027.

Our Motto: Usawa ni Sawa, Mbele Pamoja!

WHAT WE STAND FOR

MOBILISATION

To mobilise and unite teachers across Kenya in support of H.E. Dr. William Samoei Ruto's re-election bid in 2027 through civic engagement, awareness creation and voluntary electoral participation.

Unity and Solidarity

Bringing teachers together through cooperation, respect and shared purpose.

Professional Development

Supporting continuous learning, mentorship and career growth.
